## The carbon-12 scale

Atoms are far too light to weigh individually in grams, so masses are compared with the carbon-12 atom, defined as exactly 12.

- **Relative atomic mass** $A_r$ — weighted mean mass of an atom relative to $\tfrac{1}{12}$ of a ¹²C atom.
- **Relative formula mass** $M_r$ — sum of the $A_r$ values in the formula (called relative molecular mass for molecules).

```example
Find Mr of calcium nitrate, Ca(NO₃)₂. (Ca 40.08, N 14.01, O 16.00)
---
$M_r = 40.08 + 2(14.01) + 6(16.00) = 164.10$.
```

Molar mass $M$ is numerically equal to $M_r$ but has units g mol⁻¹.

## Avogadro’s law

```keyidea
At the same temperature and pressure, equal volumes of any gases contain the same number of particles. So for reacting gases, volume ratios equal mole ratios.
```

```example
What volume of oxygen reacts with 40 cm³ of methane, and what volume of CO₂ forms? CH₄(g) + 2O₂(g) → CO₂(g) + 2H₂O(l)
---
Mole ratio 1 : 2 : 1, so 80 cm³ of O₂ reacts and 40 cm³ of CO₂ forms (at the same T and p). Water is a liquid, so it is not included.
```

## Summary

- $A_r$ and $M_r$ are relative to ¹²C = 12 and have no units; $M$ has units g mol⁻¹.
- Avogadro: equal gas volumes (same T, p) contain equal numbers of molecules, so volume ratio = mole ratio.
