## Oxides across period 3

```keyidea
Metal oxides are basic, non-metal oxides are acidic, and aluminium oxide in between is amphoteric. This mirrors the change from metallic to non-metallic character across the period.
```

| Oxide | Na₂O | MgO | Al₂O₃ | SiO₂ | P₄O₁₀ | SO₃ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Character | basic | basic | amphoteric | acidic | acidic | acidic |
| Bonding | ionic | ionic | ionic/covalent | covalent network | molecular | molecular |

**With water:**

- $\text{Na}_2\text{O} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 2\text{NaOH}$ (alkaline solution)
- $\text{MgO} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Mg(OH)}_2$ (weakly alkaline)
- $\text{P}_4\text{O}_{10} + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 4\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$
- $\text{SO}_3 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$
- Al₂O₃ and SiO₂ are insoluble.

**Amphoteric Al₂O₃:** reacts with acid ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 6\text{HCl} \rightarrow 2\text{AlCl}_3 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$) and with base.

## Acid deposition and ocean acidification

- Sulfur in fossil fuels burns to SO₂; $\text{SO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{H}_2\text{SO}_3$, and SO₂ is oxidised to SO₃ → H₂SO₄.
- At high temperatures in engines N₂ and O₂ form NO, which oxidises to NO₂; NO₂ dissolves to form HNO₃ (and HNO₂).
- Effects: damage to limestone buildings, forests and aquatic life. Reduce by removing sulfur, scrubbing flue gases with CaO, and catalytic converters.
- Dissolved CO₂ forms carbonic acid, lowering ocean pH and harming shell-forming organisms.

## Oxidation states

Rules:

```figure
src: chemistry/_iitian/the-periodic-table-s3-1-6-oxidation-states-ib-dp-chemistry-s/img/fig-ccc33cec1ebd5d45.png
caption: Typical oxidation states across the periodic table; transition elements usually have several.
```

1. Elements: 0.
2. Monatomic ions: the ion charge.
3. O is −2 (−1 in peroxides); H is +1 (−1 in metal hydrides); F is always −1.
4. The sum equals the overall charge.

```example
Find the oxidation state of Mn in KMnO₄ and of S in S₂O₃²⁻.
---
KMnO₄: +1 + Mn + 4(−2) = 0 → Mn = +7.

S₂O₃²⁻: 2S + 3(−2) = −2 → S = +2.
```

Oxidation states appear in names: iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃; copper(I) chloride, CuCl; manganese(IV) oxide, MnO₂.

## Summary

- Period 3 oxides: basic → amphoteric (Al₂O₃) → acidic.
- SO₂ and NOₓ cause acid deposition; CO₂ causes ocean acidification.
- Oxidation states follow simple rules and sum to the overall charge.
