## Sensors and data loggers

| Sensor | Used for |
|---|---|
| pH probe | pH curves, following acid–base reactions |
| temperature probe | calorimetry, cooling curves |
| colorimeter | rates of reactions involving coloured species |
| pressure sensor | gas-producing reactions, gas laws |
| conductivity probe | following ion concentration |
| gas sensors (CO₂, O₂) | combustion, respiration |

```keyidea
Data loggers record many readings at high frequency and without reaction-time error — ideal for fast changes such as the steep part of a titration curve. Probes must be calibrated (e.g. pH probes against buffer solutions).
```

## Spreadsheets and databases

Spreadsheets calculate derived values (moles, concentrations, ln k, 1/T) consistently and plot graphs with trend lines. **Databases** let chemists compare IR, NMR and mass spectra with known compounds, and look up physical data.

## Modelling and simulation

Molecular modelling shows 3D shapes, bond angles and electron density; simulations model reactions, equilibria and kinetics that would be slow, dangerous or costly to test. Their predictions must be checked against experiment.

## Summary

- Sensors + loggers: many readings, high frequency, calibrate first.
- Spreadsheets for processing; databases for spectra and data.
- Models and simulations must be validated by experiment.
