  - term: Mixture

## Elements, compounds and mixtures

```keyidea
Elements are the primary constituents of matter: they cannot be broken down chemically. Compounds are elements chemically bonded in fixed ratios. Mixtures are substances physically combined — and can be separated by physical methods.
```

| | Element | Compound | Mixture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composition | One type of atom | Fixed ratio of elements | Any proportions |
| Properties | Its own | Different from its elements | Those of its components |
| Separation | — | Only by chemical reactions | By physical methods |
| Example | O₂, Fe | H₂O, NaCl | air, seawater |

Mixtures can be **homogeneous** (uniform, one phase — a solution, air, an alloy) or **heterogeneous** (non-uniform, visible boundaries — sand in water, oil and water).

## Separating mixtures

| Method | Separates | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| Filtration | Insoluble solid from liquid | Particle size |
| Crystallisation / evaporation | Dissolved solid from solution | Volatility of solvent |
| Simple distillation | Solvent from a solution | Boiling point |
| Fractional distillation | Miscible liquids | Different boiling points |
| Paper / thin-layer chromatography | Dissolved substances | Different affinity for stationary and mobile phases |
| Solvent extraction | A solute between two immiscible solvents | Different solubility |
| Magnetic attraction | Magnetic solids | Magnetism |

```example
How would you obtain pure, dry salt from a mixture of sand and salt?
---
Add water and stir to dissolve the salt; filter off the sand; evaporate some water from the filtrate and leave it to crystallise; filter and dry the crystals.
```

## Summary

- Elements: one kind of atom. Compounds: fixed ratio, chemically bonded. Mixtures: physically combined, separable physically.
- Homogeneous mixtures are uniform; heterogeneous are not.
- Choose a separation method by the property that differs.
