## Lewis acids and bases

```keyidea
A Lewis base donates an electron pair; a Lewis acid accepts one. When they react, a coordination bond forms. Nucleophiles are Lewis bases; electrophiles are Lewis acids.
```

Examples: BF₃ (Lewis acid) + NH₃ (Lewis base) → F₃B–NH₃. Ligands (H₂O, NH₃, Cl⁻) are Lewis bases that donate electron pairs to transition metal ions (Lewis acids) to form complex ions such as [Cu(NH₃)₄(H₂O)₂]²⁺.

## SN2 — primary halogenoalkanes

- **One step.** The nucleophile attacks the carbon from the side opposite the leaving group while the C–X bond breaks.
- **Transition state** with five groups around carbon (partial bonds).
- **Rate = k[RX][Nu⁻]** — second order (bimolecular).
- The configuration is **inverted** (like an umbrella turning inside out), so a chiral reactant gives the opposite enantiomer.
- Favoured by **polar aprotic** solvents (e.g. propanone).

## SN1 — tertiary halogenoalkanes

- **Step 1 (slow):** the C–X bond breaks heterolytically, forming a **carbocation** and X⁻.
- **Step 2 (fast):** the nucleophile attacks the planar carbocation from either side.
- **Rate = k[RX]** — first order (unimolecular RDS).
- A chiral reactant gives a **racemic mixture**.
- Favoured by **polar protic** solvents (e.g. water), which stabilise the ions.

```keyidea
Tertiary carbocations are stabilised by the positive inductive effect of three alkyl groups, and the crowded tertiary carbon blocks backside attack — so tertiary halogenoalkanes react by SN1, primary by SN2.
```

## Leaving group

Rate: **iodo > bromo > chloro > fluoro**, because the C–X bond enthalpy decreases down the group, so the bond breaks more easily.

```example
2-bromo-2-methylpropane reacts with aqueous NaOH. Doubling [OH⁻] has no effect on rate. Explain.
---
It is a tertiary halogenoalkane, reacting by SN1. The rate-determining step is the ionisation of the C–Br bond to form a carbocation, which does not involve OH⁻. So rate = k[RBr], zero order in OH⁻.
```

## Summary

- Lewis acid accepts, Lewis base donates an electron pair → coordination bond.
- SN2: one step, rate = k[RX][Nu], inversion; primary.
- SN1: carbocation intermediate, rate = k[RX], racemisation; tertiary.
- Leaving group ability: I > Br > Cl.
