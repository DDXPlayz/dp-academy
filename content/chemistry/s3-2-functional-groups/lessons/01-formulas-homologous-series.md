    definition: A family of compounds with the same functional group and general formula, in which successive members differ by CH₂.
  - term: Skeletal formula
    definition: A formula showing only the carbon skeleton (as lines) and functional groups; C and H atoms on carbon are not drawn.

## Ways of writing formulas — butan-1-ol

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Empirical | C₄H₁₀O |
| Molecular | C₄H₁₀O |
| Full structural | every atom and bond drawn |
| Condensed structural | CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂OH |
| Skeletal | a zig-zag of three lines ending in OH |
| Stereochemical | wedges and dashes show 3D arrangement |

## Homologous series

```keyidea
Members of a homologous series share a functional group and general formula, differ by CH₂, have similar chemical properties and show a gradual trend in physical properties.
```

General formulas: alkanes CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, alkenes CₙH₂ₙ, alcohols CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH, carboxylic acids CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH.
