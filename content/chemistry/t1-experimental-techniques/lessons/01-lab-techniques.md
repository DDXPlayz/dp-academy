## Measuring

| Quantity | Apparatus | Typical uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Mass | 2 d.p. balance | ±0.01 g |
| Volume (accurate) | pipette / burette | ±0.04–0.06 / ±0.05 cm³ per reading |
| Volume (approximate) | measuring cylinder | ±0.5 cm³ (100 cm³) |
| Making solutions | volumetric flask | ±0.1–0.2 cm³ |
| Temperature | thermometer / probe | ±0.5 °C / ±0.1 °C |

## Key techniques

- **Titration:** pipette a known volume into a conical flask, add indicator, run in titrant from a burette until the end point; repeat to concordant titres.
- **Calorimetry:** measure the temperature change of a known mass of solution in an insulated cup; extrapolate to correct for heat loss.
- **Reflux:** heat a reaction for a long time without losing volatile substances (vertical condenser).
- **Distillation:** separate liquids by boiling point (simple or fractional).
- **Recrystallisation:** purify a solid product.
- **Melting point:** check purity — pure substances melt sharply.
- **Chromatography (paper/TLC):** separate and identify components by Rf value.
- **Colorimetry:** measure absorbance to follow concentration of a coloured species (Beer–Lambert: absorbance ∝ concentration).
- **Gas collection:** gas syringe or over water (for insoluble gases).

```keyidea
Choose the technique and apparatus by the precision needed — volumetric glassware for quantitative work, measuring cylinders only for approximate volumes.
```

```example
How would you make 250.0 cm³ of 0.100 mol dm⁻³ Na₂CO₃ solution? (M = 105.99)
---
Weigh 0.02500 mol × 105.99 = 2.65 g accurately. Dissolve in a beaker of distilled water, transfer with rinsings to a 250.0 cm³ volumetric flask, make up to the mark with distilled water (bottom of meniscus at the line), stopper and invert to mix.
```

## Safety

- Wear eye protection; use a fume cupboard for toxic or volatile substances.
- Know hazard symbols: flammable, corrosive, toxic, oxidising, irritant, environmental hazard.
- Heat flammable liquids with a water bath, not a flame.
- Dispose of heavy metals and organic waste in labelled containers, not the sink.
